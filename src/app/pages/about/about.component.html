Your chefs

Desiree

Meals that taste like where you are

American, French and Vietnamese, Desiree moved to Costa Rica 30 years ago and blends her background into an inventive fusion of cuisines. She knows where to find the best local seafood and produce, and she is just as happy making old-fashioned comfort favorites.

Juan

Authentic Costa Rican hospitality, from the grill to the glass

A retired high school horticulture teacher, Juan now spends his time as a chef and bartender. He is a master of the grill and of mixology, so expect a whole fish wrapped in banana leaves or a made-from-scratch Margarita.
